About

Building the AI-first future

I'm a product leader and software builder who has spent a decade at the intersection of engineering, AI, and business strategy. I started writing code — embedded C for hardware systems, Python for machine learning models, SQL for enterprise data platforms — before moving into product leadership, where I could create more leverage by shaping what gets built rather than building it alone.

I've shipped enterprise software across the full stack: from equipment monitoring systems and cloud data pipelines to B2B SaaS platforms and generative AI products. In parallel, I've run the business side — defining go-to-market strategy, closing enterprise accounts, growing consulting revenue, and scaling teams from one person to forty-five. The result is a rare combination: I can speak fluently with engineers about architecture, with executives about strategy, and with customers about value.

Today my focus is on AI-first products, but that focus is grounded in real software craft and hard-won business experience. If you're building something serious — or figuring out how AI fits into your business — let's talk.
